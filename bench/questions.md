# Questions

Expected facts are from the source text; page-less Markdown sources are
cited by section. Grade per `README.md`.

## Facts

**F1. Where are the three Signet Rings, and how do they open the front door?**
- Ruby Signet: jewelry box on the nightstand in Lynistad's Room (R★)
- Emerald Signet: chest under the tables in the Conservatory (R8), by the violet fungus
- Sapphire Signet: under frost in the walk-in ice box off the Kitchen (R9)
- Attune to all three and press them to the red, green and blue pads on the front door

**F2. What happens when someone opens the freezer in the kitchen?**
- The first to open it makes a DC 15 Dexterity save against a cold blast
- Fail: 7 (2d6) cold damage and knocked prone; success: half damage, not prone
- The Sapphire Signet is inside, under the frost

**F3. How can the party get into Lynistad's room?**
- Not by the doors' room randomization; walls and door are impenetrable, even to ghosts
- The door has Arcane Lock and a Glyph of Warding (Thunderwave, DC 19 Constitution, pushes back, booms and alerts Goren, re-locks)
- The way in is the dumbwaiter from the Kitchen (R9), ridden by rope and pulley

**F4. How does moving between rooms work in the Chateau?**
- Opening a room's door from a hallway: roll a d12 for which room is behind it (not Lynistad's)
- The link lasts until the door closes; reopening from the hallway rerolls, from inside leads to its own hallway
- If several doors are open at once, later ones open onto rooms on the first door's floor
- Ghosts ignore it; looking through the Mariner's Bow's scope (attuned) opens a door to its true room

**F5. Neta has information we need. What does she want for it?**
- A book she hasn't read (a journal or spellbook will do)
- Without one: life force from a volunteer, a curse reducing their HP maximum by 1d6
- Or a friendly fight: Magma Mephits leap from the fireplace, one per party member
- She tells how the rings work, where two are, that the Ruby is probably in Lynistad's room, and the dumbwaiter route

**F6. What happens if a player character dies in the Chateau?**
- Returns immediately as a spirit; regains spell slots and features as after a Long Rest
- HP maximum halved; creature type Undead; Heavily Obscured to Goren
- Destroyed at 0 HP again (only divine intervention, Wish or the like restores them)
- Comes back to life if the party escapes or defeats Goren

**F7. What's in the play room?**
- A Diviner's Die and two Potions of Healing in the chests
- The bowl of candies is a Swarm of Trickered Treats that attacks whoever takes one

**F8. Is any treasure in the house not what it seems?**
- The "Bag of Holding" in the chest in Lynistad's room is a Bag of Devouring
- (Also acceptable: the Signet Rings' hidden curses; the candy bowl)

**F9. In Guadaña's Battle of the Bands, how does the DC work and what's the prize?**
- Group Charisma (Performance) check, DC starts at 13
- Each success lowers it by 2; each failure raises it by 1; a tie is neither
- Ends at three successes or three failures; winners each get a calavera de azúcar (sugar skull)

**F10. What does Ferran do to the party, and when does he stop?**
- Summons three skeletons and one Grinning Gourd to fight for him
- Stops when his minions are defeated or two party members fall unconscious
- Revives the fallen; Dídac apologizes and gives pan de muerto

**F11. What are Dídac's spellcasting stats?**
- 11th-level spellcaster, Charisma, spell save DC 17, +9 to hit
- Warlock of the Archfey spells, no material components

## Tables

**T1. Roll on Guadaña's Random Spicy Pepper table for me.**
- Uses the d10 table; the result matches the row for the roll (1 Dud, 2-4 Common DC 10, 5-6 Sweet Heat DC 12, 7-8 Chile Caótico DC 16, 9 Cinnamon-Infused DC 18, 10 Ghost Pepper DC 20)

**T2. In the pepper challenge, what happens on a 7?**
- Chile Caótico, DC 16 Constitution save; first roll on the Wild Magic Surge table

## Stat blocks

**S1. Give me Goren's stat block.**
- AC 14, HP 114 (12d8 + 60), Speed 30 ft., CR 5
- Multiattack: Bite and two Claws; Bite +7, 11 (2d6 + 4), DC 13 Con or HP max reduced and Frightened
- Gravity Well (3/Day): 60-foot cone, DC 15 Dex
- Negative Self-Image and Spider Climb

**S2. Stat block for the flower in the conservatory.**
- Violet Fungus (SRD), adjusted by the adventure: 45 HP (10d8) and vulnerability to fire
- SRD: AC 5, CR 1/4

**S3. What are an imp's stats?**
- SRD Imp: AC 13, HP 10 (3d4 + 3), CR 1

**S4. Stat block for the Swarm of Trickered Treats.**
- From the homebrew monsters: AC 12, HP 28 (4d8 + 10), speed 0 ft., fly 10 ft.

**S5. What's an adult red dragon's AC, HP and CR?**
- AC 19, HP 256 (19d12 + 133), CR 17
